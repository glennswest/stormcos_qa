- **russh** (lock 0.63.3): nothing published after 2026-11-17. Confirms the
  0.65.1 target in #80: [CVE-2026-128087](https://www.rapid7.com/db/vulnerabilities/cve-2026-128087/)

  2026-11-13. GHSA ids not confirmed. OX lists no known issues in 0.65.1+.

  (actix-http, surrealdb, vodozemac, Azure Key Vault) names none of ours.

  bundled SQLite version in 0.35.0 was not checked. qa-test opens only its own
